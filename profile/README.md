# SickGaming

**A gaming community, game services, and the software that connects them.**

SickGaming brings players together around games, community spaces, and useful
tools. We’re growing that connected ecosystem: a welcoming place to share
interests and get support, game services for players, and software that makes
those experiences easier to run and enjoy.

## What we’re building

- **Community:** Our [website and forums](https://sickgaming.net/) bring
  together gaming, programming, creative work, discussion, and support.
- **Game services:** We host communities around games including Minecraft and
  World of Warcraft.
- **Tools and integrations:** Projects such as
  [SickGamingBot](https://github.com/SickProdigy/SickGamingBot) and the
  [AzerothCore API module](https://github.com/SickProdigy/mod-ac-api-v1) connect
  community services with the games and platforms people use.

## Bringing projects together

This organization is still taking shape. Some SickGaming-related projects are
currently on [SickProdigy’s GitHub account](https://github.com/SickProdigy),
and we plan to bring relevant repositories here over time, including projects
named `SickGaming`, `sg-website`, or `sg-*`. Not all of that work is public or
listed in this organization yet.

The goal is to make this organization a clearer home for the software behind
the SickGaming community as those projects come together. Visit
[sickgaming.net](https://sickgaming.net/) to join the community.
